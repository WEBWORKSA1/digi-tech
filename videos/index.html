Why these channels

There is more technology video than anyone can watch. We picked channels that are accurate, that explain instead of hype, and that cover different ground: two for learning to build things, one for quick orientation on new tools, one for hardware you might buy, and one for research that has not reached products yet.

How the players work

Each player shows the channel's own uploads list, so it is always up to date without anyone here editing it. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play, and the players use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. The videos are hosted by YouTube and belong to their creators.
