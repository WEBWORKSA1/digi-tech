## How to use it

Pick the text colour and the background colour. The ratio, a live preview and a pass or fail for each standard update as you change them.

## What the ratio means

Contrast ratio compares how light two colours are, from 1 to 1 for identical colours up to 21 to 1 for black on white. The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, known as WCAG, set the minimums most organisations and many laws refer to:

- **AA** asks for 4.5 to 1 for normal text and 3 to 1 for large text.
- **AAA** asks for 7 to 1 for normal text and 4.5 to 1 for large text.
- Icons, form borders and other controls need 3 to 1 against what is next to them.

Large text means about 24 pixels and up, or about 19 pixels and up when bold.

## Questions people ask

### Who benefits from good contrast?

Everyone reading on a phone in sunlight, as well as people with low vision or colour blindness, which affects roughly one man in twelve.

### Is passing AA enough?

AA is the level most accessibility rules require. Treat it as the floor, and aim higher for long passages of body text.

### Does colour blindness change the result?

The ratio is based on lightness, not hue, so a pair that passes stays readable for most kinds of colour blindness. Do not rely on colour alone to carry meaning.
