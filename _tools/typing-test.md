## How to use it

Click in the box and type the passage shown above it. The clock starts on your first key and stops when you reach the end. Correct characters turn green and mistakes are underlined, so you can see where you slip.

## How the score is worked out

Typing tests count a word as five characters, including spaces, so that short and long words are treated fairly. Your speed is the number of correct characters divided by five, divided by the minutes you took. Accuracy is the share of characters you typed that matched the passage.

## Questions people ask

### What is a good typing speed?

Most adults type between 35 and 45 words a minute. Above 60 is fast, and professional typists often pass 80.

### How do I get faster?

Aim for accuracy first. Correcting a mistake costs more time than typing slowly, and speed follows once your fingers stop hesitating. Ten minutes a day without looking at the keyboard beats an hour once a week.

### Why can I not paste into the box?

Pasting is switched off so the score reflects typing.
