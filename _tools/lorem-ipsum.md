## How to use it

Set the number of paragraphs and how long each should be, then copy the text into your design. Press **Generate again** for a different arrangement.

## Why designers use placeholder text

Real sentences pull attention. When a client reads a draft layout, they start editing the words instead of judging the spacing, the type and the hierarchy. Scrambled Latin looks like natural language from a distance, with a realistic mix of short and long words, but says nothing, so the eye stays on the design.

The classic passage is based on a text by the Roman writer Cicero and has been used by printers since the 1500s.

## Questions people ask

### Does lorem ipsum mean anything?

Not as written. The words are real Latin, but they have been cut and shuffled until the meaning is gone.

### Should placeholder text ever go live?

No. Search before you publish. Leftover placeholder text is one of the most common mistakes on new websites, and it makes a page look unfinished to visitors and to ad reviewers.
