## How to use it

Type or paste into the box. The counts update live, and nothing leaves your device, so it is safe for drafts you have not published.

## What the numbers mean

- **Words** are groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks.
- **Characters** includes spaces and punctuation, which is how most social networks and form limits count.
- **Reading time** assumes 230 words a minute, a typical pace for silent reading of everyday text.
- **Speaking time** assumes 140 words a minute, a comfortable pace for a talk or a voice-over.

## Questions people ask

### How long should a web page be?

As long as it takes to answer the question properly. Search engines do not reward a particular word count. They reward pages that people find useful.

### How many words is a five minute speech?

About 700 at a relaxed pace. Paste your script and check the speaking time figure.

### Does it count words in other languages?

It works for any language that puts spaces between words. Languages written without spaces, such as Chinese and Japanese, are better measured by the character count.
