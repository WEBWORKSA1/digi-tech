## How to use it

Fill in the fields on the left. The preview shows roughly how the page will look in search results, and the code box gives you the tags to paste inside the head section of your page.

## What these tags do

- The **title** is the clickable headline in search results and the text on the browser tab. Put the main subject first.
- The **description** is the summary underneath. It does not change ranking directly, but a clear one earns more clicks.
- The **canonical** link tells search engines which address is the official one when the same page can be reached several ways.
- The **Open Graph** tags control the title, text and picture shown when the page is shared on social networks and in messaging apps.

## Lengths that work

Search engines cut titles at around 60 characters and descriptions at around 160. The counters warn you when you pass those points. Share images display best at 1200 by 630 pixels.

## Questions people ask

### Should I add a keywords tag?

No. The major search engines have ignored it for many years.

### Google shows a different description from the one I wrote. Why?

Search engines may pick another passage from the page if they judge it a better match for what the person searched for. A specific, accurate description is used more often.
