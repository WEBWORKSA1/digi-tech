## How to use it

Enter the file size and your connection speed. The large figure is the transfer time, and the table shows the same file at common broadband speeds for comparison. For uploads, enter your upload speed, which on most home connections is much lower than the download speed.

## The sum behind it

File sizes are in bytes and connection speeds are in bits per second, and a byte is eight bits. So the calculator multiplies the file size by eight and divides by the speed. A 50 GB game on a 100 Mbps line is 400,000 megabits divided by 100, which is 4,000 seconds, or about 67 minutes.

Real transfers are usually a little slower. Wi-Fi, other people on your network and the speed of the server at the other end all take their share.

## Questions people ask

### Why is my download slower than the speed I pay for?

Your plan is a maximum, measured in megabits. A download window usually shows megabytes per second, which is the same speed divided by eight. If it is slower than that, see [how to fix slow Wi-Fi](../../guides/fix-slow-wifi/).

### What speed do I need for streaming?

About 5 Mbps for HD and 15 to 25 Mbps for 4K, for each screen playing at once.
