## How to use it

Paste the page address, then fill in where the link will be shared. The finished link appears underneath, correctly encoded and ready to copy.

## What each tag records

- **Source** is where the visitor came from, such as `newsletter` or `facebook`.
- **Medium** is the type of channel, such as `email`, `social` or `cpc` for paid clicks.
- **Campaign** is the promotion the link belongs to, such as `spring_sale`.
- **Term** and **Content** are optional. Use Content to tell two links in the same message apart, for example a button and a text link.

Analytics tools, including Google Analytics, read these tags from the address and group visits by them.

## Keep your reports clean

Tags are case sensitive, so `Email` and `email` become two separate rows. Pick lowercase with underscores and stick to it. The tick box does this for you. Keep a shared list of the names you use, and never tag links between pages of your own site, because that overwrites where the visitor originally came from.

## Questions people ask

### Do UTM tags affect search ranking?

No. They only add information for analytics.

### The link is very long. Does that matter?

Not to browsers. For print or speech, put it behind a short link or a [QR code](../qr-code-generator/).
