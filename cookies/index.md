## What cookies are

A cookie is a small piece of data that a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, do the same job. They let a site remember things between pages and visits. The [glossary](../glossary/#cookie) has a one-line definition.

## What this site uses

| Type | Set by | Purpose | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preferences in local storage | Digi.Tech | Remembers your colour theme, favourite tools, best quiz score and that you closed the cookie notice | Until you clear your browser data |
| Session storage | Digi.Tech | Holds tech pulse headlines for ten minutes so pages load faster | Until you close the tab |
| Advertising cookies | Google AdSense and its partners | Show adverts, limit how often you see one, measure performance and, where permitted, personalise adverts | Set by Google, typically up to 13 months |
| Video cookies | YouTube | Set only if you press play on an embedded video | Set by YouTube |

We do not set any tracking cookies of our own, and the tools do not use cookies at all.

## Your choices

- **Advertising.** Turn off personalised advertising in [Google's Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads), or use the industry opt-out page at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked to choose before personalised advertising is used, and you can change that choice at any time from the privacy message.
- **Your browser.** Every browser lets you block or delete cookies in its privacy settings. The site and all its tools keep working if you do.
- **Local preferences.** Clearing site data in your browser resets the theme, favourites and quiz score.

More on how information is handled is in the [privacy policy](../privacy/).
