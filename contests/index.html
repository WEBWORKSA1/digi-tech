Contests and prizes

Two contests, judged on merit and free to enter. The best entries become part of the site, with your name on them.

  • Tool Idea Challenge

    Describe a small online tool you wish existed, in 150 words or fewer. Say who would use it and what it should do.

    The winner gets the round's prize, the tool built and published here, and a credit on its page.

  • Explain It Simply

    Pick any technology term and explain it in 100 words or fewer so that a twelve year old would understand.

    The winner gets the round's prize and their explanation published in the glossary with a credit.

How a round works

  1. A round opens. Its prize and closing date are posted on this page.
  2. You enter with the form. One entry per person for each contest.
  3. The editors judge every entry on usefulness, clarity and originality.
  4. The winner is contacted by email and announced here.

Current round

Round one is open for early entries. Its prize and closing date will be posted here as soon as the prize fund is confirmed, and everyone who has entered will be emailed first.

Prizes are paid for by supporters and sponsors. To fund a prize in your company's name, see sponsorship.

Rules in brief

  • Entry is free. No purchase or donation is needed, and donating does not improve your chances.
  • These are contests of skill. Winners are chosen by judging, not by chance.
  • You must be 18 or over, or the age of majority where you live. Void where prohibited by law.
  • Your entry must be your own work and must not have been published elsewhere.
  • You keep ownership of your entry. By entering you allow Digi.Tech to publish it, edited for length and clarity, with credit to you.
  • Winners are responsible for any tax due on a prize. A prize may be replaced with one of equal value.
  • The judges' decision is final. Entries that are abusive, unlawful or generated in bulk are removed.
  • Your details are used only to run the contest, as set out in the privacy policy.
  • These contests are run by Digi.Tech alone. They are not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Google, YouTube or any other company named on this site.
