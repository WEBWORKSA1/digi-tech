Where these headlines come from

Hacker News is a discussion site where programmers and founders share links and vote on them. Its front page is a reliable early signal of what the industry will be talking about next week.

DEV Community is a publishing platform for software developers. The list shows the posts readers reacted to most over the past seven days, which leans towards practical tutorials and lessons learned.

GitHub is where most open-source software is developed. The list shows projects created in the last two weeks that have gathered the most stars, a rough measure of how many developers found them interesting.

How the page stays current

Your browser asks each source for its public list when the page opens, so what you see is minutes old at most.
