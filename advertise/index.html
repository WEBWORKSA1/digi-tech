Advertise, sponsor or partner

People arrive at Digi.Tech in the middle of a task: securing an account, shipping a website, fixing a connection. That is a good moment to be the helpful name next to the answer.

Start a conversation

Ways to work together

  • Sponsor a tool

    Your name and link on a tool page your customers already use, for a fixed term.

  • Sponsor a guide or a series

    Support plain-English education on a subject close to your product. We keep editorial control.

  • Site sponsorship

    A single, clearly labelled sponsor position across the site.

  • Fund a contest prize

    Put your name on a round of the Tool Idea Challenge or Explain It Simply.

  • Build something together

    Co-develop a tool, a data set or a video series and publish it on both sites.

  • The website and domain

    Interested in acquiring or licensing this website or the Digi.Tech domain name? We are open to serious inquiries.

How we handle sponsorship

  • Sponsored positions are always labelled as such.
  • Sponsors do not review or change editorial content, and tools work the same for everyone.
  • We do not accept sponsorship for products we would not recommend to a friend, and we follow the content rules of our advertising partners.
  • Display advertising on the site is served by Google AdSense and is bought through Google, not through us.
