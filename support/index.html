Support Digi.Tech

Every tool and guide here is free, with no account and no paywall. Advertising covers part of the cost. Readers who chip in cover the rest, and decide how fast the site grows.

Where your money goes

This is the split we work to. It is a target, reviewed as the site grows.

  • Building new tools and guides30%
  • Running costs: domain, services and software20%
  • Hiring writers, developers and video makers20%
  • Promotion and marketing15%
  • Contests and prizes15%

The small print

Donations are voluntary gifts to an independent publication. They are not payments for goods or services and are not tax-deductible. Giving does not buy advertising, influence what we publish, improve your chances in a contest or change the adverts you see. We never ask visitors to click on adverts. If you donate by mistake, contact us within 14 days and we will refund it.
