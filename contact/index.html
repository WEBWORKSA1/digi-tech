What to include

  • A correction: the page address and what is wrong. We fix confirmed errors quickly and say so.
  • A tool problem: what you entered, what you expected and what happened. The browser info tool can copy your device details for you.
  • A tool or guide request: what you are trying to get done.
  • A copyright or trademark concern: the page address and the material in question. See the disclaimer.

We cannot offer one-to-one technical support or recover accounts, and we will never ask for your password.
