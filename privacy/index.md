## The short version

- The tools run in your browser. What you type, paste or open in them is not sent to us.
- We have no user accounts and we do not sell personal information.
- Advertising is provided by Google AdSense, which uses cookies.
- If you send a form, we receive what you wrote so that we can reply.

## Who we are

This site, Digi.Tech, is published by Web.Works. Reach us through the [contact page](../contact/).

## Information you give us

When you send any form on this site, such as the contact form, the starter kit request, a donation pledge, a contest entry or a job application, we receive the details you entered and the address of the page you sent it from. We use them only for the purpose you sent them: to reply, to send what you asked for, to run the contest or to consider your application.

Forms are delivered by FormSubmit, a form-to-email service, which processes the submission on our behalf. We keep messages for as long as needed to deal with them and to keep reasonable business records, and delete them on request.

If you join the update list, you can leave it at any time by replying to any message or using the contact page.

## Information stored on your device

The site saves a few settings in your browser's local storage: your colour theme, your favourite tools, your best quiz score and whether you have dismissed the cookie notice. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

## Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show adverts.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve adverts based on your earlier visits to this site and to other sites.
- Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to show you adverts based on those visits.
- You can switch off personalised advertising in [Google's Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You can also opt out of many other vendors' personalised advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised advertising is used. More detail is in the [cookie policy](../cookies/).

## Other services that load in your browser

- **Fonts** are loaded from Google Fonts.
- **The tech pulse** asks the public interfaces of Hacker News, DEV Community and GitHub for their current lists. Those services see the request from your browser, including your IP address.
- **Videos** are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. YouTube may then set cookies.
- **The QR code tool** downloads its encoder from a public code delivery network. Your text is processed on your device.
- **Hosting** is provided by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security.

Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy policy.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information we hold about you, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent. Residents of California have the right to know what is collected and to ask for deletion, and we do not sell personal information. To use any of these rights, contact us through the [contact page](../contact/). You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

This site is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contests and job applications are open to adults only.

## Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top.
