## What this site is

Digi.Tech is short for digital technology. The site does two things.

- **Tools.** Small, fast utilities for jobs that come up constantly: making a password, shrinking an image, checking a colour, reading a timestamp. They run in your browser, so what you put into them stays with you.
- **Explanations.** Guides and a glossary that describe how the technology around you works, in plain English, without assuming you already know the vocabulary.

Alongside those are a live [tech pulse](../pulse/), a set of [video channels](../videos/) we rate, and a [quiz](../quiz/).

## What we believe

**Useful beats impressive.** A tool should do one job, do it at once and get out of the way.

**Your data is yours.** The simplest way to protect what you type is never to receive it. That is why the tools work on your device.

**Clear is kind.** If an explanation needs three terms you have not met, it is not finished.

**Honest about money.** The site is funded by advertising, by [supporters](../support/) and by clearly labelled [sponsorship](../advertise/). None of them decides what we write. The [editorial policy](../editorial-policy/) sets out the rules.
