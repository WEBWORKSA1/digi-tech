    How these tools work

    Most online tools send what you type to a server, process it there and send back the result. These do not. The code is delivered to your browser once, and from then on the work happens on your own phone or computer. That makes them fast, lets them work on a poor connection, and means passwords, documents and photos you use with them are never transmitted or stored by us.
