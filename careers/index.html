Work with us

Digi.Tech is made by freelancers who work remotely, on their own schedule, and are paid for each piece we accept. If you can make technology clear, we would like to hear from you.

Who we are looking for

  • Technical writers

    You can explain how something works to a reader who has never met it, in 600 accurate words, and you check your facts.

  • Tool developers

    You write clean, dependency-free JavaScript and care about accessibility. Each tool is a small, self-contained function.

  • Video makers

    You can turn a guide into a two minute explainer with clear narration and honest visuals.

  • Community and outreach

    You know where our readers spend time online and can introduce the site there without spamming anyone.

How it works

  1. Send the form with links to work you are proud of.
  2. If it is a fit, we agree a brief, a deadline and a fee in writing before you start.
  3. You deliver, we edit together, and you are paid when the piece is accepted.
  4. Good work leads to regular commissions.

What to expect from us

  • No unpaid trial work.
  • A named credit on what you make, unless you prefer otherwise.
  • Clear briefs and one round of consolidated feedback.
  • Honesty about volume: commissions depend on the budget from advertising and supporters, so the number of openings varies from month to month.

We welcome applicants from every country and background. Read the editorial policy to see the standard we work to.
