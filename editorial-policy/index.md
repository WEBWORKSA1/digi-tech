## Accuracy

Guides and glossary entries are written from primary sources wherever they exist: standards documents, official documentation and the published guidance of recognised security and standards bodies. Figures are checked by calculation, and each tool is tested against known results before release.

Every guide shows the date it was last reviewed. Technology changes, so we revisit guides and update that date when we do.

## Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it and we do not hide that we did. Material corrections are noted on the page. Report an error on the [contact page](../contact/).

## Independence

- Advertisers, sponsors and donors have no say in what we publish and do not see content before it goes live.
- Sponsored positions are labelled. We do not publish advertising that is dressed up as editorial.
- We do not accept payment for inclusion in a guide, the glossary, the video list or the tech pulse.
- Where we mention a product or company, it is because it is the clearest example, not because of a commercial relationship.

## Advertising

Display advertising is provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners choose which adverts appear. We do not ask or encourage anyone to click on adverts, and adverts are kept visually distinct from tools and content.

## Use of AI

We use AI tools to help with research, drafting and code review. A person is responsible for everything published, checks the facts and tests the tools.

## Third-party content

The tech pulse shows headlines from public feeds and links to the original publishers. The videos page embeds players from YouTube. We select the sources, but we do not control and are not responsible for the content they publish.

## Advice

Nothing here is professional advice. Security guidance describes sound general practice and cannot account for your particular circumstances. See the [disclaimer](../disclaimer/).
