## Part 1: accounts

**1. Turn on a password manager.** Use the one built into your phone or browser, or install an independent one.

**2. Give your main email a new, unique password.** Make it with the [password generator](../../tools/password-generator/).

**3. Add two-factor authentication to that email account.** The [2FA guide](../../guides/two-factor-authentication/) shows which kind to choose.

**4. Save the backup codes** for every account with 2FA, in the password manager or on paper at home.

**5. Create a passkey** for one important account to try it. See [passkeys explained](../../guides/passkeys-explained/).

## Part 2: devices

**6. Install pending updates** on your phone, computer and browser, and switch on automatic updates.

**7. Set a screen lock** on every device, with a six digit PIN or longer.

**8. Turn on device encryption.** It is on by default on current phones. On computers, check for FileVault or BitLocker in settings.

**9. Switch on Find My Device** so a lost phone or laptop can be located, locked or wiped.

**10. Remove apps and browser extensions you no longer use.** Each one is something else that can go wrong.

## Part 3: backups

**11. Check your phone's cloud backup** is on and that the last backup was this week.

**12. Set up an external drive** with Time Machine or File History.

**13. Add an off-site copy** with a cloud backup service or a second drive kept elsewhere. The [3-2-1 guide](../../guides/backup-3-2-1/) explains why.

**14. Scan your key documents**, such as passports and certificates, and include them in the backup.

**15. Restore one file** from each backup to prove it works.

## Part 4: home network

**16. Change the router's admin password** from the one printed on the label.

**17. Give your Wi-Fi a long, unique password** and check it uses WPA2 or WPA3 security.

**18. Update the router's firmware** from its app or settings page.

**19. Create a guest network** for visitors and smart home gadgets. Share it with a [Wi-Fi QR code](../../tools/qr-code-generator/).

**20. Run a speed test by cable and by Wi-Fi.** If they differ widely, follow the [slow Wi-Fi guide](../../guides/fix-slow-wifi/).

## Part 5: privacy

**21. Review app permissions** on your phone. Remove location, microphone and camera access from apps that do not need it.

**22. Check which apps are connected** to your email and social accounts, and remove the ones you do not recognise.

**23. Search for your email address** in a reputable breach notification service, and change any password that was exposed.

**24. Tighten the privacy settings** on your social profiles, especially who can see your phone number, birthday and friends list.

**25. Learn the three signs of a scam message** in the [phishing guide](../../guides/spot-phishing/), and share them with one person who would benefit.
